Search visibility starts with clean metadata and consistent routes.

## Metadata

Each article page can generate:

- `title`
- `description`
- Open Graph data

## Sitemap

Your sitemap should at least include:

1. Homepage
2. All blog posts
3. All static pages

This MVP also includes tag routes so crawlers can discover topic archives more easily.
